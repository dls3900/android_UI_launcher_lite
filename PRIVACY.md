# Still Launcher privacy policy

_Draft. Add your name and a contact email, then host this page at a public URL and enter that URL in Play Console._

Still Launcher is a home screen app. It does not collect, send, sell, or share any personal data.

## What the app reads

- The list of apps on your device that can be opened from a home screen, including their names and icons. This is needed to show and open your apps.

## What the app stores

The following is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage:

- Your favorite apps and their order.
- The last few apps you opened from the launcher, to show the "Recent" list.
- Display settings: content position, alphabet side, and wallpaper dimming.

Removing the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all of it. If device backup is turned on in Android, these settings may be included in your own device backup.

## What the app does not do

- It has no internet permission and makes no network connections.
- It contains no ads, analytics, or third-party tracking.
- It does not read your contacts, messages, location, files, or app usage history from the system.
